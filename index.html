About

01 — OVERVIEW

I learn by building. My background is hands-on fabrication and design — welding, machining, and CAD — applied to projects I take from a rough idea all the way to a working, physical object. Currently I fabricate suspension and drivetrain components for Sacramento State's Baja SAE team, and I designed and built a hubless brushless DC motor from scratch as a senior engineering project. I'm drawn to problems that require sourcing parts, prototyping, testing, and iterating until something actually works in the real world.
